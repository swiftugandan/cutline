# UN Regulation No. 149 — headlamp photometry (passing beam and driving beam), with citations

Prepared 8 October 2026. Scope: headlamps with an asymmetrical passing beam and/or driving beam (not AFS, not the symmetric L/T-category classes AS–DS except where noted).

## Read this first: which text applies

R149 now has two series. The **01 series of amendments** (in force 4 January 2023) rewrote the headlamp classes and tables. Under the 01 series there is no Class A, B or D *passing* beam any more: a normal M/N-category car headlamp (halogen, LED or HID) is approved as **Class C passing beam** (symbol "C"), and **Class V** is a lower-output asymmetric passing beam. Driving beams are **Class B** ("HR", normal car) or **Class A** ("R", lower). Since **1 September 2026** Contracting Parties need not accept 00-series approvals first issued after that date (01 series §7.2.2), while 00-series approvals issued before that date stay accepted (§7.2.3). A checker for new designs should therefore use the 01-series tables. The 00-series tables are given in §2c and §4b for legacy approvals.

Symbol trap: under the 00 series the symbol "C" meant a **Class A** passing beam (00 §5.2 title: Class A, B, D = "C", "HC", "DC"). Under the 01 series "C" means **Class C**.

**Converting degrees to a 25 m screen (Derived, not regulation text).** The regulation states everything in degrees. On a flat screen at distance d, horizontal offset x = d·tan(h). With a vertical-polar-axis goniometer the vertical offset is y = d·tan(v)/cos(h) (exact geometry of a vertical-axis sphere projected onto a plane; my derivation). Near the axis the cos(h) term is negligible. The regulation itself uses "one per cent (0.57°)" for line B (S01 Annex 5 §3.2.1.1, p.71), i.e. the legacy points are percent-of-distance values.

- Class C has **no Imax maximum** printed; Class V has 44,100 cd. The 00-series Class D limits (Imax R 43,800 / Imax L 31,300) and the Class A/B Zone I limit are gone.
